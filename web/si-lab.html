Two signatures. Both required.

Ed25519 plus hash-based SLH-DSA-SHA2-128f for every issuer, owner and agent proof. PQ keys are pinned by the operator. No classical-only fallback.

What this does not promise.

No blanket “AI-proof” or “quantum-proof” guarantee. No biological uniqueness proof, global instantaneous revocation, unlinkability, public-key encryption upgrade or atomic blockchain delivery. HS/2 uses a standardized SLH-DSA parameter set, but this software is not a FIPS-validated module and its hybrid protocol is not a ratified standard.

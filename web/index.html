HUMANITY IS NOT LEGACY SOFTWARE.

01 · ORIGIN

The story begins with a contradiction.

Humanity built machines to save time, expand knowledge and unlock possibilities. Then the machines learned to generate faces, voices, stories, identities and decisions at a scale no individual could match.

COHIBA was born from that cultural moment — not as a rejection of AI, but as a reminder that technology only matters when people remain at the center of it.

“We are not fighting the future. We are fighting to remain human inside it.”

02 · MISSION

Turn fear into awareness. Turn awareness into community.

Our mission is to build a transparent, recognizable and community-driven digital symbol for the human side of the AI era. COHIBA uses satire, culture and blockchain transparency to create conversation around identity, automation, privacy and human agency.

03 · VISION

A global cultural signal for the age of AI.

COHIBA aims to evolve beyond a meme ticker into a recognizable internet-native identity: art, storytelling, community campaigns and open discussions about the relationship between people and intelligent machines.

The long-term vision is simple: wherever AI changes how humans live, work and express themselves, the COHIBA message remains understandable — progress without surrendering humanity.

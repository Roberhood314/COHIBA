01 / A bounded service

One workflow. Reproducible evidence.

Map the authority

Identify the agent, human principal, resource, permitted action, lifetime and revocation path. Document every route that can reach the protected effect.

Integrate a commit gate

Implement an agreed staging path around a draft or comparable local database write. Bind authorization to exact payload bytes and fail closed when authority cannot be verified.

Hand over the proof

Deliver the integration patch, positive and negative tests, redacted receipts, operating limits and a rollback guide. Pin evidence to the delivered commit.

The existing reference path is a signed draft commit in one PostgreSQL transaction. External APIs, payments, blockchain transactions and multi-system atomicity require separate design and evidence. No blanket protection or independent-audit certification is offered.

03 / Acceptance before scale

What happens after a request?

  1. Review the public brief. Confirm technical fit; arrange a private channel before sharing non-public materials.
  2. Agree the scope. Pin one operation, deliverables, schedule, acceptance tests and commercial terms.
  3. Build and test in staging. Use synthetic data and customer-approved test access.
  4. Accept the evidence. Production rollout is a separate customer decision after review.

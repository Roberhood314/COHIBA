How to qualify

Build a public contribution history first. Strong candidates show repeated original work, accurate understanding of project status, constructive community behavior and zero use of fake engagement or misleading token promotion.

30-day pilot

Week 1

Canonical-fact onboarding and contribution review.

Week 2

Deliver one original campaign contribution.

Week 3

Help onboard or support at least one contributor.

Week 4

Submit a public evidence/activity summary.

Integrity boundary

Ambassador status does not imply salary, token rewards, commissions or investment returns. No ambassador may promise price performance, guaranteed listings, unofficial contract addresses or unverified partnerships.

Application path

Contribute publicly, keep links to your work, then contact the official X account. Do not send private keys, recovery phrases or sensitive identity documents.

COHIBA ($COH)

A human-first Solana cultural crypto project exploring identity, agency and resilience in the age of artificial intelligence.

Fear the Machine. Defend the Human.
COHIBA is not positioned as anti-AI. It is a cultural response to a world in which technology accelerates while human identity, choice and authorship become increasingly important.

Project thesis

COHIBA combines meme-native storytelling with a verification-first token architecture. The project deliberately builds documentation, security controls, public evidence and failure handling before the irreversible Mainnet release.

Market integrity

COHIBA does not treat wash trading, fabricated liquidity, fake holders, circular volume or unverifiable LP-lock claims as legitimate evidence of adoption. Price, volume, liquidity, holders and market capitalization remain post-Mainnet evidence only.

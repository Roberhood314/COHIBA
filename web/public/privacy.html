Privacy

The public COHIBA website does not require users to provide a recovery phrase or private key. Wallet software remains responsible for wallet-side permissions and signing. Standard infrastructure logs may contain technical request data used for reliability and security.

Community analytics

The site may record allow-listed aggregate interaction events such as page views and clicks to official community links. The community metrics record does not store IP addresses, wallet addresses, email addresses, device fingerprints or user identifiers. Standard infrastructure/security logs may still contain technical request data according to the hosting provider's operations.

Aggregate website events are not represented as unique-human, holder or investor counts.

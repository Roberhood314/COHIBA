SI Agent

The session gate evaluates scope, effects, expiry, revocation, budget and credential epoch. The signed PoHA path supplies a verified Ed25519 proof before SI binds its decision to the exact service action.

A decision is not an execution token. The commit gate still consumes the nonce and checks current authority.

Sovereign Continuity

Research recovery preserves revocations, spent budgets, nonce history and receipt hashes. A restored ledger increments its epoch and remains fenced from execution.

Infrastructure recovery cannot regenerate human authority. Operator revalidation and fresh authorization are required.

Production deployment: signed local drafts

The local draft path checks signed PoHA, consumes nonces and saves the exact draft in one PostgreSQL transaction. Concurrent retries resolve the existing commit; a write failure rolls back its permission receipt.

Inspect production scope and operations

This bounded deployment remains pre-audit. Distributed SI, unattended recovery and arbitrary external effects are not production-certified.

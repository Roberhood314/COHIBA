# COHIBA 30-Day Content Execution Calendar

## Operating cadence
- 2–4 X posts/day.
- 1 flagship visual or meme/day.
- 1 community prompt every 2 days.
- 1 Proof, Not Promise technical post/week.
- 1 contributor spotlight/week.
- 1 long-form thread/week.
- Token/market content remains factual and minority-share before Mainnet.

## Week 1 — Establish the Human Signal

### Day 1 — Manifesto
**Flagship:** Fear the Machine. Defend the Human.  
**Angle:** COHIBA is not anti-AI; it is pro-human agency.  
**CTA:** “What should humans never outsource to machines?”

### Day 2 — Synthetic identity
**Hook:** “When anyone can generate a face, a voice and a story, what does identity mean?”  
**Asset:** Human vs synthetic face split visual.  
**CTA:** Reply with one thing that makes identity human.

### Day 3 — Meme culture
**Hook:** “Memes are not noise. They are compressed culture.”  
**Asset:** Meme about AI learning everything except why humans laugh.  
**CTA:** Remix.

### Day 4 — Proof, Not Promise
**Topic:** Why COHIBA separates PLANNED, VERIFIED and POST-MAINNET data.  
**Link:** Verification page.  
**CTA:** Verify one claim yourself.

### Day 5 — Human Signal #1
**Question:** “Would you let an AI make a life-changing decision for you if it was statistically better than you?”  
**CTA:** Quote-post with yes/no + reason.

### Day 6 — Build in public
**Topic:** Show verification/security architecture without price language.  
**CTA:** Technical feedback.

### Day 7 — Weekly recap
**Format:** What we believe / what we built / what remains unverified.  
**CTA:** Follow for next week’s Human Signal.

## Week 2 — Participation

### Day 8 — Machine vs Human #1
Challenge: Create a meme showing the moment a machine becomes more confident than its creator.

### Day 9 — Creator philosophy
Post: “COHIBA does not need people to repeat the brand. It needs people to reinterpret it.”

### Day 10 — Privacy
Question: “If convenience requires giving up privacy, where is your line?”

### Day 11 — Human-made
Feature a human-made sketch, photo, meme or short piece from the community.

### Day 12 — Proof, Not Promise
Topic: fixed supply, authority revocation and why claims should be on-chain.

### Day 13 — Meme thread
Five AI-era contradictions:
1. More automation / less time.
2. More connection / less trust.
3. More content / less certainty.
4. More intelligence / less authorship clarity.
5. More convenience / less control.

### Day 14 — Week 2 recap
Feature top replies/remixes, with permission/attribution.

## Daily post template
Every post should fit one primary goal:
- **Reach:** meme / provocative cultural idea.
- **Conversation:** Human Signal question.
- **Trust:** Proof, Not Promise.
- **Activation:** contribution ask.
- **Retention:** spotlight / recap.

Avoid mixing all five goals into one post.

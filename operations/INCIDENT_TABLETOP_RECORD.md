# COHIBA Internal Incident Tabletop Record v1.0

## Status
**INTERNAL TABLETOP CONTROL WALKTHROUGH IMPLEMENTED**

The automated operational drill executes and records four pre-Mainnet incident scenarios:
1. treasury signer/device compromise;
2. domain/social account takeover;
3. corrupt/partial launch state;
4. dependency/runtime production outage.

Each scenario is checked against mandatory response actions derived from the Incident Response Runbook.

## Evidence
Generated CI evidence:
- `operations/evidence/operational-drill.json`
- `operations/evidence/operational-drill.md`

The CI artifact is the authoritative dated execution evidence.

## Boundary
This is an internal preparedness drill, not an independent security assessment. A future human tabletop may add named participants, elapsed response times, communications evidence and corrective-action owners.

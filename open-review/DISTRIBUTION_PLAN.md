## Response discipline
- Respond to substantive issue/review within 24 hours where possible.
- Never pressure a reviewer to soften findings.
- Credit external contributors when requested.
- Do not claim “partner” or “auditor” merely because outreach was sent.
- Publish remediation evidence after sensitive findings are safe to disclose.

# COHIBA ($COH) — Project Profile

## Short description
COHIBA ($COH) is a Solana community meme project exploring humanity, identity and resilience in the age of artificial intelligence. Its theme, **“Fear the Machine. Defend the Human.”**, uses satire and internet culture to spark discussion about automation, synthetic identity, privacy and human agency.

## Medium description
COHIBA is a human-first community meme project built on Solana. The project was created around a cultural tension of the AI era: technology can accelerate knowledge and creativity, while also creating concerns around automation, identity, surveillance and control. COHIBA turns that tension into an internet-native symbol through satire, storytelling, transparent token design and community participation.

The token design targets a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 COH, 9 decimals, 0% token tax, immutable metadata, and permanent revocation of mint and freeze authorities after verified initial issuance.

## Mission
**Turn fear into awareness, and awareness into community.**

## Vision
Become a recognizable internet-native cultural signal for people who believe technological progress should move forward without surrendering human identity, dignity and agency.

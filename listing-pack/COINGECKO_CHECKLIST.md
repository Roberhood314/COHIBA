## Standard tracked listing after launch
CoinGecko requires the asset to be actively tradable on a supported exchange/market for a standard listing.

- [ ] Official Mainnet mint
- [ ] Explorer URL
- [ ] Active supported market
- [ ] Organic liquidity/trading activity
- [ ] Public verification post
- [ ] Accurate supply data
- [ ] Logo and metadata
- [ ] Official social channels
- [ ] Direct trade URL
- [ ] Submit via official Self-Serve Request Form

Avoid artificial volume and listing-request spam.

## Policy
- 0% token tax.
- No hidden minting after launch.
- Mint and freeze authorities are permanently revoked after verified initial issuance.
- Allocation transfers should use clearly labeled public wallets.
- Team/development allocations should use a disclosed vesting/timelock policy before public market launch.
- Liquidity policy and LP-lock/burn decision must be published before claiming liquidity is locked.

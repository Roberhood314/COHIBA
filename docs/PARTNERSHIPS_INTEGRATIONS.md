# COHIBA Partnerships & Integrations Framework v1.0

## Objective
Turn partnerships into verifiable integrations or shared audience value — not logo collection.

## Partner categories
1. **Solana infrastructure** — RPC, analytics, explorers, wallets, security.
2. **Security/research** — independent review, monitoring, incident response.
3. **Creators/media** — AI culture, digital identity, internet culture, design.
4. **Community ecosystems** — regional Solana/crypto builder communities.
5. **Data/listing** — market-data platforms after eligibility.
6. **Consumer/community products** — tools that can use COHIBA identity, community or verification data.

## Partnership maturity states
- IDENTIFIED
- CONTACTED
- DISCOVERY
- TECHNICAL_EVALUATION
- PILOT
- LIVE_VERIFIED
- INACTIVE

Never label a relationship “partner” before at least PILOT unless the other party has explicitly approved public use of that term.

## Integration evidence
A LIVE_VERIFIED integration should have at least two of:
- working public URL/API integration;
- public announcement by both parties;
- code or configuration evidence;
- partner dashboard/listing page;
- measurable referral/integration activity.

## Outreach thesis
COHIBA offers:
- a clear AI-era culture thesis;
- a public verification/data layer;
- community/content collaboration;
- open-source integration surface;
- transparent pre-Mainnet status.

## Partner discovery questions
- What mutual user/community problem is solved?
- What exactly will be integrated or co-produced?
- Which party owns implementation?
- What is public evidence of launch?
- How will success be measured?
- Are there regulatory/brand restrictions?
- Can either party terminate/disable the integration?

## Initial targets to explore
These are target categories, not claimed relationships:
- Solana security researchers/firms;
- Solana community organizations;
- wallet/explorer/data providers;
- AI/identity/privacy creators;
- meme/visual creators;
- market-data platforms when Mainnet/market eligibility exists.

## Partnership register schema
For every target, record:
`name | category | status | owner | contact channel | proposed value | last interaction | next action | public claim allowed? | evidence URL`

## Public claim rule
No fabricated partnerships, no unilateral logo use, and no “integration” claim without implementation evidence.

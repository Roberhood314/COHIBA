# COHIBA Culture System v1.0

## Purpose
COHIBA culture exists to turn a technical crypto project into a recognizable human signal in the AI era.

The culture must survive even if token price, market attention or speculative demand disappear.

## Cultural territory
**Human identity in the age of AI.**

COHIBA does not compete for attention by copying generic meme formats. Its cultural territory is the tension between:
- human creativity and machine generation;
- identity and synthetic identity;
- agency and algorithmic control;
- privacy and surveillance;
- speed and meaning;
- automation and human dignity.

## Core line
**Fear the Machine. Defend the Human.**

Supporting lines:
- We are not fighting the future. We are fighting to remain human inside it.
- Human First.
- The Human Signal.
- Trust the chain, not a promise.
- Memes move culture. Evidence builds trust.

## Cultural principles
1. **Human First** — human agency remains the center of the story.
2. **Culture With Meaning** — memes should carry an idea, not only a joke.
3. **Open Participation** — community members can create, remix and interpret the narrative.
4. **Verification Over Hype** — technical and market claims must be reproducible.
5. **No Manufactured Community** — no fake followers, fake engagement or purchased activity represented as organic adoption.
6. **No Anti-Technology Extremism** — COHIBA critiques loss of agency, not technology itself.
7. **No Price Worship** — token price is not the project identity.

## Content pillars
### 1. Human vs Machine — 35%
Memes, images, short commentary, cultural observations and provocative questions about AI-era identity.

### 2. Internet Culture — 20%
Meme-native formats, satire, community remix, reaction content and cultural references.

### 3. Build in Public — 20%
Real engineering updates, verification evidence, security work, Mainnet readiness and transparent blockers.

### 4. Community Signal — 15%
Community art, contributor spotlights, translations, debates, memes and collaborative campaigns.

### 5. Token / Market — 10%
Only verified token facts, release gates and post-Mainnet market evidence.

## Tone
- intelligent but internet-native;
- provocative without becoming hostile;
- concise;
- visual;
- skeptical of hype;
- confident only where evidence exists.

Avoid:
- guaranteed-return language;
- fake urgency;
- “100x”, “moon guaranteed”, “next DOGE” claims;
- fabricated partnerships;
- unverified market statistics;
- copying another project’s identity.

## Community rituals
### Human Signal
Weekly community prompt: one question about AI, identity, privacy, creativity or agency.

### Machine vs Human
Recurring meme/art challenge with community voting based on creativity, not token holdings.

### Proof, Not Promise
Weekly technical transparency post linking to real evidence.

### Creator Signal
Feature one community member, meme, artwork, translation or technical contribution.

### Open Build
Monthly public review of what was built, what failed and what is next.

## Contributor paths
Community members can contribute through:
- meme creation;
- visual art;
- short-form video;
- translation/localization;
- research;
- moderation;
- security review;
- documentation;
- development;
- ecosystem introductions.

Contribution recognition should initially use public attribution and reputation, not automatic token rewards.

## Culture health indicators
Track:
- percentage of organic replies;
- unique creators per month;
- contributor retention;
- number of community-originated posts;
- ratio of cultural content to price content;
- repeat participation in weekly rituals;
- number of languages/local communities represented.

Follower count alone is not a culture KPI.

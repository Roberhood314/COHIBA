## Growth quality
- Organic engagement ratio = organic engaged accounts / organic impressions.
- Contributor retention = returning contributors / contributors from prior period.
- Creator ratio = unique creators / active community participants.
- Community content ratio = community-created assets / total published assets.

## Anti-gaming rule
Do not count purchased followers, bots, duplicated identities, wash interactions or artificial traffic as organic community growth.

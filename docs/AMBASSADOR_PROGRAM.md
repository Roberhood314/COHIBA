# COHIBA Ambassador Program v1.0

## Position
A COHIBA ambassador is a trusted contributor and local cultural operator — not a paid shiller.

## Purpose
Expand COHIBA into new creator networks, languages and communities while preserving canonical facts and project integrity.

## Entry criteria
A candidate should demonstrate at least three of:
- repeated original contributions;
- accurate understanding of project status;
- original audience or community access;
- translation/localization ability;
- creator, research, technical or moderation capability;
- constructive behavior under disagreement;
- no history of fake engagement or misleading promotion.

## Disqualifiers
- guaranteed-return language;
- fake follower/engagement services;
- posting unofficial contract addresses as official;
- fabricated partnerships;
- undisclosed impersonation;
- harassment;
- repeated factual misrepresentation.

## Pilot roles
### Culture Ambassador
Memes, art, short-form culture and creator recruitment.

### Language Ambassador
Translation, local context and regional community development.

### Builder Ambassador
Developer onboarding, open-source contributions and technical education.

### Trust Ambassador
Scam reporting, verification education and misinformation correction.

## 30-day pilot
Week 1: onboarding and canonical-fact check.  
Week 2: one original campaign contribution.  
Week 3: onboard or support at least one new contributor.  
Week 4: submit activity/evidence summary.

## Evaluation
- quality of contribution;
- factual accuracy;
- contributor retention generated;
- organic reach quality;
- collaboration behavior;
- zero integrity violations.

## Compensation policy
No token, salary, commission or reward is implied by ambassador status unless a future written program explicitly states terms. Any paid campaign must be disclosed and measured separately from organic community activity.

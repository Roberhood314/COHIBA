## Jurisdictional baseline — Vietnam
Vietnam's Resolution 05/2025/NQ-CP, effective 9 September 2025 for a five-year pilot, regulates pilot offering/issuance, crypto-asset trading-market organization and crypto-asset services in Vietnam.

Important points from the official Government text:
- issuers in the pilot are Vietnamese LLCs or joint-stock companies;
- pilot-issued crypto assets must be based on real underlying assets, excluding securities and fiat;
- such crypto assets are offered/issued only to foreign investors under the pilot;
- relevant trading/services must use Ministry of Finance-licensed crypto-asset service organizations;
- participants must comply with AML/CFT, cybersecurity, information security, electronic-transactions and data-protection requirements;
- disclosure must be accurate, complete, timely and not misleading.

Official source:
https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-quyet-so-5-2025-nq-cp-ve-trien-khai-thi-diem-thi-truong-tai-san-ma-hoa-tai-viet-nam-119250909184045221.htm

## COHIBA implication
COHIBA should **not** assume that a public token presale or fundraising campaign marketed from Vietnam is legally equivalent to an ordinary offshore meme-token launch.

Before any fundraising, token sale, private allocation for consideration, or Vietnam-facing marketing campaign, external counsel should determine:
1. issuer/entity jurisdiction;
2. whether the activity falls inside Vietnam's pilot regime;
3. whether COH structure is compatible with the pilot's underlying-asset requirement;
4. permitted investor classes;
5. marketing/advertising restrictions;
6. AML/KYC obligations;
7. tax treatment;
8. consumer/investor disclosures;
9. cross-border restrictions;
10. sanctions and restricted-jurisdiction screening.

## Fundraising structure decision tree
### A. Equity/SAFE into an operating company
Requires company/entity setup, securities/corporate-law review, investor documents and tax advice.

### B. Grants/sponsorship
Document scope, deliverables, IP, conflicts, disclosure and whether funds create token-related expectations.

### C. Token sale/private token allocation
Highest legal sensitivity. Do not execute before jurisdiction-specific written legal review.

### D. Community participation
Non-financial contribution/recognition is currently the lowest-complexity growth path; do not imply future compensation unless formally approved.

## Compliance documents required before institutional fundraising
- legal entity profile;
- beneficial ownership/cap table;
- founder/team identity package for counterparties;
- IP ownership/assignment;
- privacy policy and data map;
- Terms/Risk Disclosure;
- sanctions/AML policy appropriate to activity;
- conflict-of-interest policy;
- treasury policy;
- fundraising use-of-proceeds;
- token-rights analysis;
- tax memo;
- jurisdictional legal opinion or counsel memo.

## Listing integrity
CoinMarketCap's current criteria emphasize credibility, verifiability, functional website/explorer, real public trading and project representatives. CoinGecko requires official verification steps and evaluates project maturity, team presence and liquidity/organic attention.

Official references:
- https://support.coinmarketcap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043659351-Listings-Criteria
- https://support.coingecko.com/hc/en-us/articles/23725417857817-Verification-Guide-for-Listing-Update-Requests-on-CoinGecko
- https://support.coingecko.com/hc/en-us/articles/4498809321369-Why-is-my-token-not-listed-on-CoinGecko

## Hard compliance gates
Before fundraising/listing claims:
- no guaranteed return language;
- no fake volume/holders/liquidity;
- no unverified partnership claims;
- no unlicensed public offering assumption;
- no “audited” claim without independent report;
- no circulating-supply claim without methodology;
- no privacy claim inconsistent with actual infrastructure logs;
- no Mainnet/market claim before on-chain evidence.

## Accepted evidence
- public GitHub issue/PR/contribution from a non-maintainer;
- independent security review inquiry or finding;
- community-created art/video/translation/research;
- qualified external mention;
- ambassador output;
- verified referral/partner introduction;
- dated official X analytics export.

## Rejected evidence
Bots, purchased followers, duplicated identities, self-generated circular engagement, fabricated partnerships and unverifiable screenshots are excluded.
